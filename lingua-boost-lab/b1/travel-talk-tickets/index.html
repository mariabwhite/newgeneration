4b

Prepositions · transport & arrive

Two rules that always confuse learners. Read once, then drill the 10 MCQ below.

in — small & enclosed
in a car · in a taxi · in a lorry
any vehicle where you're inside a private box.
on — public & large
on a train · on a bus · on a plane · on a ship · on a bike
public transport + anything you can walk around on.
on board
on board the ship · on board the plane
formal alternative for larger vehicles.
by — how you travel
by car · by train · by plane · by bike · by foot
describe the mode of travel — no article.

🎯 arrive + preposition

arrive at
arrive at the station · at the airport · at the hotel · at the museum
small, specific place — a point on the map.
arrive in
arrive in London · in Paris · in Italy · in the countryside
big place — a city, a country, a region.
Trap: we say arrive home — no preposition. And after «get», we say get to the station / to London (both places take «to»).

🎯 Choose the right preposition · 10 items

1. We travelled ___ a taxi to the airport.

2. The children were ___ the school bus when the rain started.

3. We had dinner ___ the plane.

4. They danced ___ the ship all night.

5. How did you get to work today? — I came ___ bike.

6. The train arrived ___ Paddington Station on time.

7. We arrived ___ Rome late at night.

8. The kids finally arrived ___ safe and sound.

9. Please put your bag ___ the overhead bin ___ the plane.

10. We prefer to travel ___ train — you can walk around, eat, read.

Role-Play Lab · four scenarios

Four real ticket-booth situations. Read your role only, then play out loud with your partner or teacher.

🎭 Card 1 · Buy a ticket for the exhibition
Role A · Buyer
  • 🎯 Goal: buy one ticket for the exhibition.
  • ❗ Problem: no change is available.
  • Useful phrases: «I'd like one ticket for the exhibition, please.» · «Sorry, I only have a large note.» · «Do you accept cards?» · «Can I pay online or get change somewhere?»
Role B · Seller
  • Explain payment options: cards, contactless, mobile pay.
  • If none work — direct to nearest ATM or online booking.
  • Stay polite even when the buyer is stuck.
🎭 Card 2 · Guided tour for tomorrow
Role A · Buyer
  • 🎯 Goal: buy a ticket for tomorrow's guided tour.
  • ❗ Problem: not sure if tickets are still available.
  • Useful phrases: «Is there a guided tour tomorrow?» · «Are there any tickets left?» · «I'd like to reserve in advance.» · «How much is it for adults / students?»
Role B · Booking staff
  • Check «system»: 3 slots left for 11:00, 4 slots for 15:00.
  • Explain price: €12 adult, €7 concession.
  • Take name and phone number for reservation.
🎭 Card 3 · Wrong date on the ticket
Role A · Buyer
  • 🎯 Goal: fix the mistake with the date on your ticket.
  • ❗ Problem: booked for the 18th, but the ticket says the 28th.
  • Useful phrases: «I booked a ticket for the 18th, but it says the 28th.» · «Can I change it?» · «Is there any way to get a refund or rebook?» · «It must be a mistake — I have the receipt.»
Role B · Support agent
  • Ask for the booking reference + receipt screenshot.
  • Explain the fix: free rebook within 24 hours if it was a system error.
  • Apologise, offer a small compensation (10 % off next booking).
🎭 Card 4 · Foreign tourist
Role A · Tourist
  • 🎯 Goal: buy a basic entrance ticket.
  • ❗ Problem: don't understand ticket types.
  • Useful phrases: «Do you speak English?» · «What kinds of tickets are available?» · «I just want a basic entrance ticket.» · «Can you explain the options?»
Role B · Cashier
  • Speak clearly, give simple options: adult / concession / audio guide.
  • Recommend the audio guide for a first visit.
  • Point out the map + free brochure.
Drill order: read your role silently → play out loud once slowly → switch roles → play again fluently without the script. ~5 min per card.

Museum Discussion · pick a story

🎫 ✅ Story 1 · Smooth booking

A person booked their museum ticket online in advance and walked straight in — no queue, no problem.

  • How did the person buy the ticket?
  • What helped everything go smoothly?
  • Have you ever booked museum tickets online? Was it convenient?
  • What are the advantages of booking in advance?
  • What kind of museums do you usually visit?
  • Do you prefer going alone or with a guided tour?
🎫 ❌ Story 2 · Sold out on arrival

Someone travelled a long way, arrived at the museum — and tickets had all sold out.

  • Why couldn't they get the ticket?
  • What could they have done differently?
  • Have you ever had a similar experience — arriving somewhere and not getting in?
  • Do you think museums should always keep a few tickets for walk-in visitors?
  • What would you do if you travelled far and couldn't get in?
  • Would you stay and wait or change your plans?
🎫 ⏳ Story 3 · The waiting queue

Someone is standing in a queue, the museum app has crashed, tickets are «still available» — but nothing loads.

  • What problems did the person face?
  • Why was the situation stressful?
  • What would you do — wait, ask for help, or give up?
  • Have you ever had technical problems while buying tickets?
  • Do you think paper tickets are more reliable than digital ones?
  • How could the museum improve its system?

Reading · Full car rental scene (Sang Min Lee)

Sang Min walks in without a reservation. Read carefully — 6 comprehension questions below.

Agent: How can I help you today?

Sang Min: I would like to rent a car.

Agent: Do you have a reservation?

Sang Min: No.

Agent: That's alright. Let's see what we can find. We have several cars to choose from. What size are you looking for?

Sang Min: What are my options?

Agent: A compact car, midsize car, and a minivan.

Sang Min: How much are they?

Agent: Compact is $21.95 a day, midsize is $27.95 a day, and a minivan is $35.95 a day.

Sang Min: I'll take a midsize car.

Agent: How long will you be renting the car?

Sang Min: One week.

Agent: How many people will be driving the car?

Sang Min: Just myself.

Agent: Would you like insurance?

Sang Min: How much is it?

Agent: It is $14.95 a day and it covers everything regardless of fault.

Sang Min: Yes please.

Agent: Can I have your name?

Sang Min: Last name is Lee. L E E. First name is Sang. S A N G.

Agent: Your address please?

Sang Min: I am from Korea. Would you like my address in Korea?

Agent: Just the city name.

Sang Min: Seoul, Korea.

Agent: Can I see your driver's license and a credit card?

Sang Min: Sure. Here it is.

Agent: Great. That will be $324.92. Would you like me to charge it on this card?

Sang Min: Yes. That will be fine.

Agent: Please sign here. You can pick up your car downstairs. Your expected time to return the car is June 29th. Show the attendant this invoice. The gas tank is full, you should fill it up before you return. If you don't want to, we can do it for you for $3 a gallon.

Sang Min: That will be all. Thank you.

Writing · Complaint letter · missing ticket

Read the model letter (B1–B2). Then write your own version — a small change of details is enough.

Topic: No ticket received after payment

Dear Museum Staff,

I'm writing to complain about a problem with my recent ticket purchase. I paid for a ticket online on July 14th, but I didn't receive any confirmation or ticket by email. The money was taken from my card, and I have the receipt.

I am very upset because I was planning to visit with my family and we couldn't enter.

Please check the issue and send the ticket or refund the money.

Regards,
Natalie Sh

✏️ Your turn — 6 lines to complete

  1. Change the date of purchase and the event (concert / museum / train).
  2. Explain the problem in your own words (QR failed / wrong date / no confirmation / seat missing).
  3. Say why it's important to you (birthday / family visit / work trip).
  4. Ask for a clear action (refund / rebook / send ticket).
  5. Keep it polite — no CAPS, no rude words.
  6. Sign with your first name only.
Speaking version: read your complaint aloud as if you were on the phone with support. 60–90 seconds. Practise the polite opener, the problem, the request.
