Reading mini · The TikTok Dance GenerationReading + Vocab

Short text · 4 MC + 2 T/F · vocabulary in context

Ten years ago, a serious dancer's path was clear: years of rehearsal, ruthless auditions, and a hope that one day you'd see your name on an opening-night programme. Today, that path is no longer the only one — and for many teenagers, it isn't even the obvious one.

Sixteen-year-old Lera from Saint Petersburg has never been to a casting call. Yet, her short clips of contemporary choreography have been watched over three million times. «I post once a week,» she says, «but I rehearse every day. The difference is — my audience is the phone, not the theatre.»

Critics worry that this shift produces a generation of dancers who can deliver a brilliant fifteen-second hook but can't sustain a full ninety-minute performance. Defenders reply that visibility is what matters — and a smartphone gives more visibility in a month than a regional theatre could give in a decade.

Lera herself is more cautious. «If I had to choose one thing forever, I'd choose the stage. Online, you go viral and you're forgotten in a week. On stage, people remember you for years.» She pauses. «But for now — I take both.»

Grammar focus · Wishes & ConditionalsGrammar drill

On the theme of stage life · type the right form into each gap

Wish + Past Simple — for a present regret. «I wish I had more time to rehearse.»
Wish + Past Perfect — for a past regret. «I wish I had auditioned last year.»
2nd Conditional — present unreal. «If she were more confident, she'd post more.»
3rd Conditional — past unreal. «If I had uploaded earlier, it would have gone viral.»

Fill the gaps

1. I wish I more rehearsal time before opening night. (present regret · have)

2. If she a little more confident on stage, she a lot more followers. (present unreal · be / gain)

3. I wish I that performance video sooner — now everyone has moved on. (past regret · upload)

4. If I for the school musical, I would never have discovered my passion. (past unreal · not audition)

5. He wishes he perform in front of a real audience instead of a phone camera. (wish + could · ability)

6. If they her earlier, she it to the national stage by now. (3rd cond · support / make)

7. If only I so nervous before going live — it ruins my voice. (if only · be · negative)

8. I wish my parents how much dance matters to me. (present regret · understand)

Speaking Task 3 · Describe ONE photo1.5 min prep · 2 min talk

5-point plan · talk for 2 minutes · don't stop · use linkers
5-point plan
  1. Where & when the photo was taken
  2. What is in the foreground — describe the dancer in detail
  3. What is happening — what's the moment?
  4. Why you chose this photo to show your friend
  5. What you feel when you look at it
tap to start preparation --:--
Sample answer (open AFTER you've tried it yourself!)
I've chosen this photo because it captures a moment I find really powerful. It looks like it was taken backstage, just before a live performance — probably in a theatre, late in the evening, judging by the soft lighting. In the foreground we can see a young dancer in full costume. She is standing very still, almost as if she's holding her breath. Her hands are clasped in front of her — a classic gesture of someone trying to calm their nerves. What is happening is the moment right before she steps on stage — those final seconds when everything you've rehearsed has to come together. You can almost feel the silence. I decided to share this photo with you because I know how much you love the theatre, and I think this image captures something words can't — the private second before the public one. When I look at it, I feel a mix of admiration and a little bit of stage fright myself. It reminds me that behind every performance there's a human being who is just as nervous as we would be.

Speaking Task 4 · Compare TWO photosHigh level · 10 points

Project work · 2.5 min prep · 3 min talk · 6 points to cover

Project topic: "Live performance vs. social media: how do young artists today choose where to share their talent?"

You've found these two photos for the project. Imagine you're recording a voice message to your project partner.

6 obligatory points
  1. Brief description of each photo (2 sentences each, link to project)
  2. Differences (2 key contrasts)
  3. Advantages of each (1-2 each)
  4. Disadvantages of each (1-2 each)
  5. Which one you'd prefer — and why
  6. Conclusion tying back to the project topic
tap to start preparation --:--
Full sample answer with linkers (open AFTER you've tried it!)
Hi! I've found two great photos for our project on «Live performance vs. social media», and I'd like to share my thoughts on them. The first photo shows a young dancer in the middle of a performance on a real stage. The audience is in the dark, and all the lighting is on her — a classic theatre moment. The second photo shows a dancer at home, filming a short clip with a ring light, presumably for social media. Both photos illustrate the topic perfectly because they show the two main «stages» a young artist can choose today. In terms of differences, the most obvious one is the audience: in the first photo, the audience is physically present, while in the second one, the audience is virtual and invisible to the performer. Another important difference is preparation: a stage show takes weeks of rehearsal, whereas a viral clip can be made in an afternoon. Speaking of advantages, performing live builds real-time connection with people and develops resilience under pressure. The screen, on the other hand, offers far greater reach — a single video can be seen by millions, which would be impossible in any theatre. However, both have drawbacks too. Live performances are limited to one city and one night, and stage fright can be brutal. Online content, in turn, is forgotten very quickly, and the algorithm doesn't care how talented you are if your first three seconds don't hook the viewer. If I had to choose, I'd personally prefer the stage. The energy of a live audience is something a screen cannot replicate, and I believe artistic depth grows from those high-stakes moments. To conclude, both formats are valid, but they shape very different kinds of artists — and the smartest performers of our generation are probably the ones who learn to live in both worlds.

Self-check · Task 4 criteriaMaximum 10 points

Tick each box after you re-listen to your recording

K1 · Решение коммуникативной задачи (max 4)

  • ✓I mentioned the project topic in the introduction If the topic isn't named, examiners take 1 point off immediately
  • ✓I described both photos (2 phrases minimum per photo) Not just listed — described in connection with the topic
  • ✓I gave at least 2 differences Not «one is on stage, one is online» — that's one difference
  • ✓I gave advantages AND disadvantages for both (1-2 each) Forgetting disadvantages = guaranteed 1 point off
  • ✓I stated my own preference and why «I'd choose…» + reason. "I prefer" instead of "I'd prefer" is a language error
  • ✓I included a conclusion tying back to the project No conclusion = 1 point off for organisation

K2 · Организация высказывания (max 3)

  • ✓I used intro + body + conclusion structure clearly Listener should hear "Hi!" / "Speaking of…" / "To conclude"
  • ✓I used at least 5 different linkers However, in contrast, on the other hand, speaking of, in terms of…
  • ✓No big silent pauses longer than 3 sec Use "well…", "let me think…" rather than silence

K3 · Языковое оформление (max 3)

  • ✓No more than 3 serious lexico-grammatical errors Serious = changes the meaning. Wrong tense in a key phrase, wrong word
  • ✓No more than 3 phonetic errors that distort meaning Don't confuse th/s/z, /v/-/w/, -ed endings
  • ✓I used B2+ vocabulary from the lesson (not just B1) "audience", "rehearsal", "viral", "mainstream", "authentic" — must appear
Top 5 traps to watch for:
  1. Spelling — особенно «-ity», «-ence/-ance», double letters (successful, beginning)
  2. Word class — «success» vs «successful» vs «successfully» в word formation
  3. Wrong tense в речи под давлением — повторяем «would» в опциях, «had» в past wishes
  4. Skipping intro/conclusion в Task 4 — каждое = минус балл
  5. Repeating linkers («also, also, also») — bank: however / in contrast / on the other hand / speaking of / in terms of / what's more
