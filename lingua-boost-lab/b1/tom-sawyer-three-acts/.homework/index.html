G.1 · Past Simple · type the correct form · 10 items

Regular verbs add -ed. Irregular verbs — memorise: go/went, see/saw, tell/told, run/ran, think/thought, stand/stood, find/found, swear/swore, take/took, know/knew.

1. Yesterday Tom (go) to the fence with a brush.

2. The boys (see) the murder in the graveyard.

3. Aunt Polly (tell) him to paint the fence.

4. They (run) away to a small island.

5. The town (think) the boys were dead.

6. Tom (stand) up as a witness.

7. Tom and Becky (find) the way out of the cave.

8. The boys (swear) a blood oath.

9. Injun Joe (escape) through the window.

10. Becky (forgive) Tom in the end.

G.4 · Sentence transformation · direct → reported · 8 items

Direct: "I am tired," he said. Reported: He said (that) he was tired. Tense shifts back (Present → Past, Past → Past Perfect).

1. Tom said, "I am clever." → Tom said (that) he clever.

2. Aunt Polly said, "I need help." → Aunt Polly said (that) she help.

3. Becky said, "I will not forgive you." → Becky said (that) she forgive him.

4. Tom said, "I have seen the murder." → Tom said (that) he the murder.

5. The judge said, "Muff Potter is innocent." → The judge said (that) Muff Potter innocent.

6. Tom said, "I will help him." → Tom said (that) he help him.

7. Injun Joe said, "I killed the doctor." → Injun Joe said (that) he the doctor.

8. Becky said, "I am scared." → Becky said (that) she scared.

G.8 · Translation · Ru → En · grammar focus · 10 items · reveal

Say each sentence out loud, then click Reveal for the target grammar structure.

1. [Past Simple] Том покрасил забор за один день.
Tom painted the fence in one day.
2. [Past Continuous] Когда Бен пришёл, Том красил забор.
When Ben arrived, Tom was painting the fence.
3. [Present Perfect] Ты когда-нибудь видел настоящее кладбище ночью?
Have you ever seen a real graveyard at night?
4. [Article the] Они жили у реки Миссисипи.
They lived by the Mississippi River.
5. [Preposition at] Убийство случилось в полночь.
The murder happened at midnight.
6. [Comparative] Пещера была опаснее кладбища.
The cave was more dangerous than the graveyard.
7. [Modal should] Том знал, что должен помочь Маффу Поттеру.
Tom knew (that) he should help Muff Potter.
8. [Conditional 1] Если Том расскажет правду, Мафф будет свободен.
If Tom tells the truth, Muff will be free.
9. [Conditional 2] Если бы я была Бекки, я бы простила Тома раньше.
If I were Becky, I would forgive Tom sooner.
10. [Reported speech] Том сказал, что он видел убийство.
Tom said (that) he had seen the murder.
