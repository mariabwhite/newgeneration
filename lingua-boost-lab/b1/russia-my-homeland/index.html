History · dates & rulers 🏛️

Key historical events + four iconic rulers.

Timeline + bio cards

Key dates

988
Baptism of Rus' — Prince Vladimir the Great accepted Orthodox Christianity, shaping Russian culture for centuries.
1147
First mention of Moscow in chronicles — Prince Yuri Dolgoruky founded the small fortress.
1703
Foundation of Saint Petersburg — Emperor Peter the Great built the new capital "window to Europe".
1917
Russian Revolution — end of the empire, beginning of the Soviet era.
1961
Yuri Gagarin became the first human in space.
1991
Collapse of the USSR — Russia became the modern Russian Federation.

Four iconic rulers

Ivan IV (the Terrible)

1530-1584 · first Tsar
  • First ruler to be crowned "Tsar of All Russia"
  • Expanded the country eastward (Siberia)
  • Known for his harsh rule and unpredictable temper

Peter I (the Great)

1672-1725 · westernizer
  • Westernized Russia · built modern army and navy
  • Founded Saint Petersburg in 1703
  • Brought European architecture, science and customs

Elizabeth of Russia

1709-1761 · arts patron
  • Promoted arts, music and theatre
  • Founded Moscow University in 1755
  • Brought Baroque architecture to Saint Petersburg

Nicholas II

1868-1918 · last Emperor
  • The last Emperor of Russia
  • Executed with his family in 1918 during the Revolution
  • Canonised by the Russian Orthodox Church in 2000

Famous Russians · biographies 👤

Read each short biography aloud. Then say which fact was new to you.

5 biographies · read + discuss

Alexander Pushkin

1799-1837 · poet
  • Born in Moscow into a noble family
  • Composed his first poem at age 8
  • Published "Ruslan and Lyudmila" in 1820 and became famous
  • Created literary Russian language
  • Died after a duel
  • Best-known work: "Eugene Onegin" (1823–1831)

Peter Tchaikovsky

1840-1893 · composer
  • Born in a village near modern Izhevsk
  • Composed symphonies, operas and ballets
  • Operas: "Eugene Onegin", "Iolantha", "The Queen of Spades"
  • Ballets: "Swan Lake", "Sleeping Beauty", "The Nutcracker"
  • International Tchaikovsky Competition started in 1958

Isaac Levitan

1860-1900 · landscape painter
  • Born in August, had two sisters and a brother
  • Began painting in 1873
  • Had to earn his own living after his parents died (1875, 1877)
  • Famous work: "Autumn Day. Sokolniki Park" (1879)
  • Chekhov called him "Russia's best landscape painter"

Georgy Zhukov

1896-1974 · marshal
  • Born into a poor shoemaker's family
  • At 11 went to Moscow to find a job
  • Became a general in 1940
  • Led armies at Moscow, Stalingrad, Leningrad, Kursk
  • Hero of the Soviet Union four times

Yuri Gagarin

1934-1968 · first cosmonaut
  • Born in a village near Smolensk
  • One of four children in a big family
  • Finished a school of aviation and Air Force Academy
  • 12.04.1961 · made the first space flight on "Vostok"
  • Flight around the Earth lasted 1 h 48 min

Reading aloud · ~10 lines

Moscow is the capital and the largest city of Russia. The city stands on the Moskva River in Central Russia, with a population estimated at over 13 million residents. It is one of the world's largest cities, being the most populous city entirely in Europe. Moscow is not only the political but also the cultural and economic centre of Russia. It is famous for the Kremlin, Red Square, the Bolshoi Theatre, and many other attractions. Millions of tourists come here every year to enjoy its history, architecture and modern life.

Target: 100–120 words · 5 paragraphs · answer all 3 questions words: 0
Moscow, 24 June 2026 Dear James, Thank you for your recent email. It was great to hear that you'd like to travel to Russia. To answer your questions, I usually spend my holidays at our family dacha in the countryside or sometimes in Saint Petersburg with my grandparents. The best season for travelling in Russia, in my opinion, is summer — the days are long, the weather is warm, and there are festivals everywhere. As for attractions, I would definitely recommend Red Square and the Kremlin in Moscow, the Hermitage and Peterhof in Saint Petersburg, and Lake Baikal if you want true nature. By the way, how long can you stay? Will you come alone or with your family? And are you planning to visit just Moscow or other cities too? Hope to see you here soon! All the best, Daniella

OGE Speaking · Task 3 · Monologue 🇷🇺

2-minute talk on Russia · use the 3 prompts below.

Monologue · 12+ phrases · 2 min
Topic · Russia, my homeland

"Imagine you are doing a project «Russia, my homeland». You have found some information and would like to share it with your friends."

Speak about:

  1. The most beautiful place in Russia you have visited or would like to visit;
  2. A famous Russian person (writer, composer, scientist, sportsman…) and why you admire them;
  3. One Russian tradition or holiday that you consider especially important and why.
Your turn — record your 2-min monologue:
Hi everyone! Today I'd like to share what I have found about my country, Russia. First of all, the most beautiful place I have ever visited is Lake Baikal. It is the deepest lake in the world, and the water is so clean that you can see the bottom even when it is very deep. The mountains around the lake make the view truly amazing. Secondly, the Russian person I admire most is Pyotr Tchaikovsky. He is one of the greatest composers in world music history. His ballets — "Swan Lake", "Sleeping Beauty" and "The Nutcracker" — are performed in theatres all over the world. His music sounds emotional and very Russian at the same time. Finally, the tradition that I consider especially important is Victory Day on the 9th of May. It is the day when we remember our great-grandparents who fought in the Second World War. There are parades, concerts, and the famous "Immortal Regiment" march. I think it is important because it keeps our family memory alive. So in conclusion, Russia is a country with stunning nature, brilliant people and meaningful traditions — and I'm proud to call it my homeland. Thank you for listening!
