Grammar Arcade Mission

Two grammar machines are broken: NOW CAMERA and RESULT DETECTOR. To complete the arcade, you must learn the difference between the two tenses.

Present Continuous

Action in progress now or around now.

Present Perfect

Result, experience, or unfinished time — connected to now.

Complete 12 arcade challenges and write your own Arcade Report.

Quick Grammar Map

Present Continuous
Form
am / is / are + V-ing
Use
action happening now / around now / planned future
Signals
now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!, today
Example
I am reading now.
Present Perfect
Form
have / has + V3
Use
experience, result, unfinished time
Signals
ever, never, just, already, yet, recently, so far
Example
I have finished the task.
Careful!

today, this week, this month can go with both tenses.

  • Process: I am working a lot this week.
  • Result: I have finished three tasks this week.

Ask: process now or result so far?
