08 Prefixes — Negative & Meaning-Changing

theory + drill

Negative

un- unhappy; in- incorrect; im- before m/p (impossible, immature); il- before l (illegal); ir- before r (irrational).

Reverse / Remove

dis- disagree, disrespect; de- deactivate, deforest.

Time / repetition / absence

re- rewrite (again); pre- preview (before); non- non-stop (absence).
