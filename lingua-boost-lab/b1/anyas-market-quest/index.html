Part 1 Ј Past Simple Ч finished actions with a clear time

? Past Simple Ј regular verbs Ч add -ed

Formula verb + -ed
e.g. walk > walked, ask > asked, smile > smiled
When to use it A finished action in the past with a clear time. Time words: yesterday, last Saturday, this morning, two days ago, in 2019. Same form for I / you / he / she / we / they.
Market examples Ј ?
"Yesterday Anya walked into the bakery just after seven."
"The baker smiled and handed her a warm loaf."
"Anya asked for three tomatoes and paid twelve roubles."
?? Spelling tips Verb ends in -e > just add -d (smile > smiled, bake > baked).
Verb ends in consonant + y > y > i + ed (try > tried, carry > carried).
Short verb, stressed final syllable > double the consonant (stop > stopped, drop > dropped).

? Past Simple Ј irregular verbs Ч just remember them

Formula verb > V2 (irregular)
e.g. go > went, buy > bought, take > took, eat > ate, see > saw
When to use it Exactly the same job as Past Simple regular Ч a finished past action with a time Ч but for verbs that don't add -ed. About 100Ц200 verbs. You just learn them.
Market examples Ј ?
"Anya went to the Saturday market at seven." (go > went)
"She bought a loaf, a bunch of parsley and three peaches." (buy > bought)
"The fishmonger caught her eye and held up a huge silver salmon." (catch > caught, hold > held)
"Anya took the smallest tomato and gave the rest back." (take > took, give > gave)
?? Anya's mini-list Ч the 12 you need today go > went Ј buy > bought Ј take > took Ј give > gave Ј eat > ate Ј see > saw Ј run > ran Ј smell > smelt / smelled Ј pay > paid Ј bring > brought Ј catch > caught Ј hold > held

? Past Simple Ј negatives Ч didn't + base form

Formula did not / didn't + verb (base form!)

? NOT "didn't bought"
? "didn't buy"
When to use it To say something did NOT happen in the past. Works for regular AND irregular verbs the same way Ч because did already carries the past. The main verb goes back to its base form.
Market examples Ј ?
"Anya didn't buy any cucumbers." (NOT didn't bought)
"The greengrocer didn't have fresh basil this morning."
"I didn't bring enough change, so I had to run to the ATM."

? Past Simple Ј questions Ч Did + subject + base form?

Formula Did + subject + verb (base) ?
Wh-word + did + subject + verb ?

? NOT "Did you bought?"
? "Did you buy?"
When to use it Ask about a finished past action. Same rule: did already carries the past, so the main verb is in its base form.
Market examples Ј ?
"Did you go to the market yesterday?"
"What did you buy at the bakery?"
"How much did the salmon cost?"
"Where did you get that goat's cheese?"
? Special case Ј was and were The verb to be is different Ч it does NOT use did.
Positive: I / he / she / it was Ј you / we / they were (e.g. "The tomatoes were ripe.")
Negative: wasn't / weren't (e.g. "The bread wasn't crispy.")
Question: Was / Were + subject? (e.g. "Were the peaches juicy?")
Same for state verbs like could, had Ч no auxiliary did needed in questions/negatives.

Part 2 Ј Two Past siblings Ч Past Continuous Ј used to

? Past Continuous Ч was / were + Ving Ч action in progress in the past

Formula was / were + verb-ing

I / he / she / it was
you / we / they were
When to use it Х An action that WAS IN PROGRESS at a specific past moment ("At 9 am I was choosing the flowers.")
Х A background scene ("The sun was shining, birds were singing.")
Х Two actions running together in the past Ч often with while ("While Anya was choosing the flowers, an old man was singing.")
Х A longer action interrupted by a shorter one Ч with when ("Anya was paying when her phone rang.")
Market examples Ј ?
"At 9 am Anya was choosing the sunflowers."
"While Anya was paying, her phone rang." (long + short)
"The old man was singing softly and a cat was sleeping on the newspapers." (two backgrounds)

? used to Ч a past habit or state that is NOT true anymore

Formula used to + verb (base)
negative: didn't use to
question: Did Е use to Е?
When to use it Something that happened OFTEN in the past Ч but doesn't happen now. Or something that was TRUE in the past Ч but isn't true now. Perfect for childhood memories, grandma stories, old habits.
Market examples Ј ?
"When Anya was little, her grandmother used to send her to this stall every Saturday." (habit)
"She used to buy a lollipop with the change and never tell mum."
"This market didn't use to have a cheese counter Ч it opened only two years ago."
? The split-second rule Ј Past Simple vs Past Continuous Ask yourself: "Am I telling WHAT HAPPENED or SETTING THE SCENE?"
> WHAT HAPPENED (one clear action, one clear time) > use Past Simple ("Anya bought a loaf.")
> SCENE / IN THE MIDDLE OF SOMETHING > use Past Continuous ("Anya was choosing flowers when it started to rain.")
Bonus: used to = a past habit that is NOT true now. Only for the past Ч for present habits use usually / every day.

The Old Baker's First Loaf

Yesterday morning, at four o'clock, the old baker walked into his bakery and lit the oven. He didn't turn on the lights Ч he never used to. When he was a boy, his own grandfather used to bake bread by lamp light, and the old baker still liked the soft orange glow of the fire on the wooden shelves.

While he was mixing the sourdough, a small brown mouse ran across the floor and stopped near his boot. He didn't move. The mouse looked at him, blinked once, and disappeared behind the flour sack. The baker smiled. His grandfather used to say a bakery without a mouse was a bakery without soul.

At exactly ten minutes past seven, Anya walked in. The baker was pulling the very first loaf out of the oven. It was crispy, golden, and it smelled of childhood. He gave it to her without saying anything. She didn't have enough change, but she offered him what she had. He shook his head. "Take it home," he said. "I baked this one first, and I made it for you."

Anya walked out of the bakery holding the warm loaf against her chest like a small living creature. Behind her, the baker was already starting the second batch. The mouse was watching him from the shelf. Outside, the market was just beginning to wake up.
