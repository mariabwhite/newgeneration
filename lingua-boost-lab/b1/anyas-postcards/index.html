For or Since? · 10 quick taps

⏳ Drill · how long?
FOR + a length of time (three weeks, five years, ages). SINCE + a point in time (Monday, 2019, I was 8). Tap one — green is right.
1. Anya has been travelling ___ 2019.
→ "2019" = a moment in time → SINCE
2. She has been walking around the bazaar ___ three hours.
→ "three hours" = a length → FOR
3. They have been protecting this island ___ the 16th century.
→ "the 16th century" = a starting point → SINCE
4. The vendor has been keeping that map ___ forty years.
→ "forty years" = a length → FOR
5. I haven't seen Anya ___ my last birthday.
→ "my last birthday" = a moment → SINCE
6. The tea has been ready ___ ten minutes.
→ "ten minutes" = a length → FOR
7. Anya has been writing in her diary ___ this morning.
→ "this morning" = a starting moment → SINCE
8. I have wanted to visit Kyoto ___ ages.
→ "ages" = a length (a long time) → FOR
9. We have been friends ___ I was eight.
→ "I was eight" = a moment → SINCE
10. The musician has been playing the oud ___ twenty minutes.
→ "twenty minutes" = a length → FOR
5

The Marrakech bazaar · what do you say?

🎲 Choose your path
There's no wrong choice here. Every answer is human — but each one reveals a different value, and a different way to use your new words.
The scene: You're in the bustling Marrakech bazaar. The smell of saffron and mint tea. A vendor in his sixties opens a small wooden box and shows you a beautifully drawn old map. "It belonged to my grandfather," he says quietly. "I have been keeping it for forty years. Two hundred dirhams." You pause. Three things you could say to him:
A
"Where did your grandfather travel? Tell me about him."
Loyalty · верность You honour the story behind the object. The vendor lights up — he has been waiting all morning for someone to ask. He tells you about his grandfather's caravan. You leave with the map AND a story. Loyalty means caring about where things — and people — come from.
B
"Is this really one hundred years old, or are you charming me a little?"
Honest · честный You ask the truth, gently. He laughs — a long, easy laugh. "Eighty-two years," he says. "Maybe seventy-five." He likes that you didn't pretend. You haggle, fairly. Both of you smile. Honesty doesn't kill the deal — it makes it real.
C
"It must have so many stories in it…"
Spirit · дух You don't even ask about price. You hold the map and imagine the lives that have been touching it. The vendor watches you carefully and finally says: "Take it. One hundred. Because you can already see it." That's the spirit of an object — and of a place. You felt it.

👀 Notice the tenses

"I have been keeping it for forty years"→ Present Perfect Continuous · duration
"It has been standing for fifteen centuries"→ Present Perfect Continuous · since the past, still true

Pick the right tense · 6 mini-questions

🎯 Practice · Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous
Quick check. Has done = it's finished, look at the result. Has been doing = how long it's been going on (and maybe still going). Tap one button per row — green = right, red = try the other.
1. Anya ___ four cities already.
→ result/score, finished action = Present Perfect
2. She ___ in Marrakech for three days.
→ duration ("for three days") + still there = Present Perfect Continuous
3. The vendor ___ that map for forty years.
→ "for forty years" + still keeping = Present Perfect Continuous
4. Look! Anya ___ a postcard from Bali!
→ "just" + we see the result now = Present Perfect
5. They ___ this island for centuries.
→ "for centuries" — long duration that continues = Present Perfect Continuous
6. I ___ to Kyoto twice in my life.

Anya's Marrakech diary · pick the right tense

📔 Mixed tenses · 10 gaps
Anya wrote this in a café between two glasses of mint tea. Help her pick the right verb form for each gap — Past Simple, Present Simple, Present Continuous, Present Perfect, or Present Perfect Continuous.

Day 6 · Marrakech. "We (1) here three days ago. The bazaar (2) the loudest place I have ever heard. I (3) three small treasures already — a brass lantern, mint tea, and a tiny camel. Right now I (4) in a café by the square. A musician (5) the oud for twenty minutes already. I (6) the lamb tagine for the first time today and it was magnificent. The vendor in the bazaar said his grandfather (7) that old map for forty years. Tomorrow we (8) for Cape Town. I (9) my bag already. I (10) a piece of my heart in this city for sure."

1. We ___ here three days ago.
→ "three days ago" = finished moment → Past Simple
2. The bazaar ___ the loudest place I have ever heard.
→ general truth about a place she's in now → Present Simple
3. I ___ three small treasures already.
→ "already" + result you can count → Present Perfect
4. Right now I ___ in a café by the square.
→ "right now" + action in progress → Present Continuous
5. A musician ___ the oud for twenty minutes already.
→ "for twenty minutes" + still playing → Present Perfect Continuous
6. I ___ the lamb tagine for the first time today and it was magnificent.
→ "it was magnificent" tells us it's finished → Past Simple
7. The vendor said his grandfather ___ that old map for forty years.
→ "said" (past reporting) + ongoing duration → Past Perfect Continuous
8. Tomorrow we ___ for Cape Town.
→ planned future arrangement → Present Continuous
9. I ___ my bag already.
→ "already" + completed action with present result → Present Perfect
10. I ___ a piece of my heart in this city for sure.
→ a personal prediction about tomorrow → Will-future
