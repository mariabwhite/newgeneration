03

Tenses · recognition · 10 sentences

Read the sentence and choose the tense it is in. Past Simple vs Past Continuous vs Past Perfect (with one Present Perfect distractor).

Recognition · 3 past tenses + 1 present perfect distractor
1. Yesterday I went hiking with my friends. → tense?
→ "yesterday" — finished past time + irregular verb went → Past Simple.
2. While I was preparing the report, the manager called me. → tense of the first clause?
→ "while" + was/were + -ing → Past Continuous (background action).
3. By 5pm, the team had already finished the schedule. → tense?
→ "by 5pm" + "had already" + past participle → Past Perfect (action completed before another past moment).
4. Last quarter our sales grew by 15%. → tense?
→ "Last quarter" — finished past time → Past Simple.
5. When you arrived, I was still checking the labelling. → tense of the second clause?
→ "was still + -ing" — ongoing action when something interrupted it → Past Continuous.
6. Before the meeting started, we had divided the supplies. → tense of the second clause?
→ "before … started" + had + past participle → Past Perfect (earlier of two past actions).
7. I have just finished the elaboration of my idea. → tense?
→ "have just + past participle" → Present Perfect (NOT past — the result is connected to now). Watch out for the trap!
8. The crediting policy changed last week. → tense?
→ "last week" — finished past period + regular verb -ed → Past Simple.
9. While she was sharing the costs, her tutor explained the rubric. → tense of the first clause?
→ "while + was + -ing" → Past Continuous (longer background action; "explained" is the interrupting Past Simple).
10. She had already left when I arrived at the bank. → tense of the first clause?
→ "had already + past participle" → Past Perfect (she left BEFORE I arrived).
04

Tenses · substitution · 12 sentences

Choose the correct form of the verb. Past Simple / Past Continuous / Past Perfect. Explanation appears after the click.

Substitution · 3 past tenses
1. While I ___ to the office, my phone rang.
→ "while" + длительное действие, прерванное кратким → Past Continuous (was walking).
2. By the time the bank opened, the queue ___ to the corner.
→ "by the time" = к моменту прошлого → Past Perfect (had stretched).
3. Yesterday morning I ___ the schedule with my partner.
→ "yesterday morning" — законченное действие в прошлом → Past Simple.
4. Last summer we ___ in Italy for two weeks.
→ "last summer" + завершённое действие → Past Simple.
5. While the manager ___ the rubric, the trainee was taking notes.
→ Два параллельных длительных действия → оба в Past Continuous.
6. After we ___ the contract, the celebrations began.
→ "after" + первое из двух прошлых действий → Past Perfect (had signed).
7. When I was a student, I ___ as a tutor on weekends.
→ "when I was a student" — период в прошлом, регулярное действие → Past Simple.
8. The screen shows that you ___ the report ten minutes ago.
→ "ten minutes ago" = точная точка в прошлом → Past Simple. С "ago" — никогда Present Perfect.
9. Before I joined the company, I ___ never ___ in logistics.
→ "before … joined" + "never" в прошлом → Past Perfect (had never worked).
10. While she ___ the labelling, the system crashed.
→ "while" + длительное прерванное действие → Past Continuous.
11. The CEO resigned because she ___ already ___ her main goals.
→ Причина в прошлом, ДО другого прошлого действия → Past Perfect (had already achieved).
12. Last Friday I ___ at the ATM machine for twenty minutes.
→ "Last Friday" + завершённое действие → Past Simple.

Past tenses · classify · 12 sentences

Tap a sentence, then tap the correct bin: Past Simple, Past Continuous, or Past Perfect.

Quick rules: Past Simple = finished action in the past (yesterday / last week / in 2020). Past Continuous = was/were + -ing, ongoing action at a past moment. Past Perfect = had + V3, action completed BEFORE another past action ("had already", "by 5pm", "before he came").
Yesterday he opened the bank. She was talking on the phone. We had finished by 5pm. Last week I called my tutor. The meeting was going on when I arrived. They had signed the contract before lunch. She studied logistics for two hours. He was sharing his screen. I had already checked the schedule. Mary worked at the bank in 2020. We were dividing the supplies. The team had divided the tasks before noon.

07

Irregular verbs · V2 vs V3 · 8 sentences

V2 (past form) is used in Past Simple (I went). V3 (past participle) is used after had in Past Perfect (I had gone). Pick the right form.

1. Yesterday I ___ to the bank to check my account. (go)
→ Past Simple → V2 → went.
2. By the time we arrived, he had ___ to the manager. (speak)
→ Past Perfect ("had + V3") → spoken.
3. She ___ the early bus to work last Monday. (take)
→ Past Simple → V2 → took.
4. They had ___ lunch before I joined them. (eat)
→ Past Perfect → V3 → eaten.
5. I ___ the report last night and sent it at midnight. (write)
→ Past Simple → V2 → wrote.
6. The CEO had ___ us a clear deadline before she resigned. (give)
→ Past Perfect → V3 → given.
7. We ___ at a small café near the bank. (meet)
→ Past Simple of meet → V2 → met.
8. When my tutor entered, I had already ___ the rubric. (read)
→ V3 of "read" written the same but pronounced [red] — Past Perfect: had read.

08a

Word formation · -ing / noun / verb · 10 sentences

Choose the correct form of the word in brackets. Some need -ing, some need a noun form, some — verb.

1. Modern ___ uses AI to plan delivery routes. (logistic)
→ Field name as subject → logistics.
2. Without a strict ___ policy, the bank lost millions last year. (credit)
→ Participle as adjective → crediting policy.
3. We took up ___ as a family hobby last summer. (hike)
→ Gerund as object of "took up" → hiking.
4. Examiners told me to focus on ___ in my next essay. (elaborate)
→ Noun form after preposition "on" → elaboration.
5. Let's ___ the team into two groups before the meeting. (split)
→ Bare infinitive after "let's" → split.
6. Better ___ on EU products would solve half of our delivery problems. (label)
→ Gerund as subject → labelling.
7. Try to keep a steady ___ during the IELTS speaking part. (pace)
→ Noun "rate/speed" after "a steady" → pace.
8. Without higher ___, his promotion would be impossible. (educate)
→ Noun form after "higher" → education.
9. Can you ___ the next meeting for Wednesday morning? (schedule)
→ Bare infinitive after "can you" → schedule (verb).
10. A clear voice keeps your message ___ even at high speed. (intelligible)
→ Adjective complement after "keeps … message" → intelligible.
