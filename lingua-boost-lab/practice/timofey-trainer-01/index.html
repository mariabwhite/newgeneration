03

Tenses · recognition · 10 sentences

Read the sentence and choose the tense it is in. Trains noticing the form.

Recognition · Present Simple/Cont/Perfect · Past Simple
1. She works in private banking. → tense?
→ Habitual fact, no time marker → Present Simple.
2. I am preparing the report right now. → tense?
→ "right now" + am + -ing → Present Continuous.
3. We have already checked the schedule. → tense?
→ "have already" + past participle → Present Perfect.
4. Last quarter our sales grew by 15%. → tense?
→ "Last quarter" — finished past time → Past Simple.
5. Banking requires a strict environment. → tense?
→ General truth → Present Simple.
6. She is sharing the costs with her partner. → tense?
→ am/is/are + -ing → Present Continuous.
7. My tutor has explained the rubric three times. → tense?
→ "has + past participle" + result up to now → Present Perfect.
8. Yesterday I went hiking with my friends. → tense?
→ "yesterday" → Past Simple.
9. Enterprise logistics covers warehouses and shipping. → tense?
→ General definition → Present Simple.
10. The bank has tightened its crediting policy. → tense?
→ "has + past participle" → Present Perfect.
04

Tenses · substitution · 12 sentences

Choose the correct form of the verb. After click — explanation appears.

Substitution · 4 tenses mixed
1. I usually ___ to work by car, but today I'm walking.
→ "usually" = регулярное действие → Present Simple (go).
2. She ___ a presentation right now — don't disturb her.
→ "right now" = действие в момент речи → Present Continuous.
3. Last quarter our sales ___ by 15%.
→ "last quarter" = законченный период в прошлом → Past Simple.
4. I ___ already ___ the schedule for next week.
→ "already" + результат now → Present Perfect.
5. My tutor ___ me yesterday that I should focus on speaking.
→ TELL someone (told me); SAY (to someone) — no direct "me".
6. Banking ___ a strict regulatory environment.
→ Общее правило / факт → Present Simple.
7. When I was a student, I ___ as a tutor on weekends.
→ "When I was a student" = период в прошлом → Past Simple.
8. Look! He ___ into oncoming traffic again!
→ "Look!" = action happening now → Present Continuous.
9. Logistics ___ the backbone of international trade for centuries.
→ "for centuries" + state up to now → Present Perfect.
10. Last Friday I ___ at the ATM machine for twenty minutes.
→ "Last Friday" — finished past → Past Simple.
11. Right now my colleagues ___ a new logistics model.
→ "right now" + plural subject → Present Continuous.
12. The labelling system ___ recently to match new EU rules.
→ "recently" + result now → Present Perfect.

-ing forms · 3 functions

Tap an -ing phrase, then tap the correct bin: gerund (= noun), participle (= adjective), continuous (= part of a tense).

3-in-1 rule: one ending -ing can be a gerund (noun: Hiking is fun), a participle (adjective: a charming café) or part of a Continuous tense (I am preparing). Open the scheme in the notes block above if you forget.

07

said vs told · 6 sentences

TELL someone something · SAY (to someone) something. Remember from today's lesson: said me ❌ → told me ✓.

1. My tutor ___ me to focus on speaking.
→ told me (с прямым me — это TELL).
2. She ___ that crediting policies will tighten.
→ said that... (без прямого me — это SAY).
3. Can you ___ me the schedule, please?
→ tell me.
4. He ___ to me he wanted a higher band score.
→ said to me (с предлогом TO — это SAY).
5. Could you ___ me about your last job?
→ tell me.
6. Anna ___ that the response was intelligible.
→ said that...

08a

Word formation · -ing / noun / verb · 10 sentences

Choose the correct form of the word in brackets. Some need -ing, some need a noun form, some — verb.

1. ___ is the backbone of international trade. (logistic)
→ Field name → logistics.
2. The bank tightened its ___ policy. (credit)
→ Participle as adjective → crediting policy.
3. ___ is my favourite weekend activity. (hike)
→ Gerund as subject → Hiking.
4. Examiners reward ___ of ideas. (elaborate)
→ Noun form after "reward" → elaboration.
5. Could you ___ the bill, please? (split)
→ Bare infinitive after "could you" → split.
6. ___ errors cause shipping delays. (label)
→ Gerund acting as adjective → Labelling errors.
7. She speaks at a slow ___. (pace)
→ Noun "speed/rate" → pace.
8. Higher ___ opens doors. (educate)
→ Noun form → education.
9. The taxi driver was very ___. (honest)
→ Adjective after linking verb "was" → honest.
10. Speak clearly so that you stay ___. (intelligible)
→ Adjective after "stay" (linking verb) → intelligible.
