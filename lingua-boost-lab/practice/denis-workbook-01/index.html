03

Simple · Continuous · will · going to · 12 sentences

Four tenses, one exercise, no easy pattern. Read the whole sentence, look for the signal, then choose. Explanation appears after each click.

Signals — quick: every / usually / always / never / on weekends → Simple · now / at the moment / right now / Look! → Continuous · I think / probably / OK, I… → will · tomorrow / next week / evidence (clouds, sign) → going to.
12 questions · Simple / Continuous / will / going to
1. Right now, I ___ a hoodie.
→ "right now" → this moment → Continuous · am wearing.
2. Every morning I ___ coffee.
→ "every morning" → habit → Simple · drink.
3. Look at those clouds — it ___ rain.
→ Visible evidence (clouds) → going to. NOT "will" — will = prediction без фактов.
4. — It is cold. — OK, I ___ close the window.
→ Decision made in the moment → will (NOT "going to" — план не был заранее).
5. My brother ___ in the city centre.
→ Fact + 3rd person singular → lives.
6. Tomorrow I ___ visit my parents — the tickets are ready.
→ Уже спланировано (билеты!) → going to.
7. I think our city ___ be more modern in 50 years.
→ "I think" → subjective prediction → will.
8. At the moment they ___ a movie.
→ "at the moment" → Continuous → are watching.
9. They ___ open a new café near my house — I saw the sign.
→ Есть evidence (знак) → они уже планируют → are going to.
10. On weekends we usually ___ to the park.
→ "on weekends" + "usually" → habit → Simple · go. NOT "are going" — не про этот момент.
11. Wait! I ___ help you with the bags.
→ Spontaneous offer в момент разговора → will.
12. Look! He ___ a new jacket today.
→ "Look!" → this moment → Continuous · is wearing.

07

Café dialogue · Can / Could / Like / Prefer · 8 choices

Same café phrases you already know. Choose the polite / correct option.

Register: Can I = ok · Could I / May I = more polite. I like = general. I would prefer / I'd prefer = choose one over another.
8 choices · café + polite forms
1. ___ I have a latte, please?
→ Polite request → Could I have.
2. I ___ tea to coffee in the morning.
→ "prefer X to Y" = ставим одно выше другого → prefer.
3. Do you ___ herbal tea?
→ После Do you — базовая форма → like.
4. ___ I pay by card?
→ Разрешение / возможность → Can I.
5. Sorry, we ___ gluten-free cake today.
→ Отрицание к have got → haven't got.
6. I ___ wearing sneakers to formal shoes.
→ После prefer → -ing вариант ("prefer wearing") без "to". «prefer to» ≠ то же самое.
7. ___ I have the bathroom key?
→ Просьба у продавца → Can I.
8. I don't ___ crowded places.
→ После don't — базовая форма → like.
08

Future · will vs going to · 8 sentences

The freshest topic (28 June). Will = decision at the moment / prediction. Going to = plan already made / obvious from what you see.

Quick rule: "OK, I will help you" (decide now) · "I am going to study English tomorrow" (already planned). "Look at the sky — it is going to rain" (evidence).
8 questions · will vs going to
1. — It is cold. — OK, I ___ close the window.
→ Решение в момент разговора → will.
2. I ___ visit my parents this weekend — the tickets are ready.
→ Заранее спланировано → am going to.
3. Look at those clouds — it ___ rain.
→ Очевидное по фактам (тучи) → is going to.
4. I think our city ___ be more modern in 50 years.
→ Прогноз / мнение → will ("I think").
5. Next month I ___ move into a new flat.
→ План уже принят → am going to.
6. Wait! I ___ help you with the bag.
→ Спонтанное предложение в момент → will.
7. They ___ open a new cafe near my house — I saw the sign.
→ Уже планируют (evidence — знак) → are going to.
8. Probably it ___ be crowded on the weekend.
→ "Probably" → предположение / прогноз → will.
09

Frequency & prepositions · 8 sentences

Two things at once. Frequency words stand BEFORE the main verb (but AFTER to be). Prepositions of time: at 8 o'clock · in the morning · on weekends · during the day.

Position: I usually drink coffee (before verb) · I am usually tired (after to be) · Usually, I drink coffee (comma, at start).
8 questions · frequency + at/in/on/during
1. I ___ get up at 7 a.m.
→ Frequency word ПЕРЕД main verb → usually get up.
2. My brother ___ tired in the evening.
→ Frequency word ПОСЛЕ to be → is often.
3. I meet my friends ___ 6 p.m.
→ Точное время → at.
4. I usually cook ___ the evening.
→ Часть дня → in the morning / afternoon / evening.
5. We go to the park ___ weekends.
→ Дни / weekends → on.
6. He works ___ the day and rests at night.
→ "day" / "week" / "night" (продолжительный отрезок) → during.
7. I ___ drink cappuccino.
→ Frequency word ПЕРЕД глаголом → rarely (rare = прилагательное).
8. The bus is very busy ___ 8 in the morning.
→ Точное время (8) → at.
