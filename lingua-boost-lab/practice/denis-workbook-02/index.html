03

Simple · Continuous · will · going to · 12 sentences

Four tenses, one exercise, no easy pattern. Read the whole sentence, look for the signal, then choose. Explanation appears after each click.

Signals — quick: every / usually / always / never / on weekends → Simple · now / at the moment / right now / Look! → Continuous · I think / probably / OK, I… → will · tomorrow / next week / evidence (clouds, sign) → going to.
12 questions · Simple / Continuous / will / going to
1. Right now, my wife ___ the lease with the landlord.
→ "right now" → this moment → Continuous · is signing.
2. Every morning I ___ the subway downtown to my office.
→ "every morning" → habit → Simple · take.
3. Look at that thick cloud over Lake Ontario — it ___ snow.
→ Visible evidence (thick cloud) → going to. NOT "will" — will = prediction без фактов.
4. — My phone died again. — OK, I ___ lend you my SIM card.
→ Decision made in the moment → will (NOT "going to" — план не был заранее).
5. My landlord ___ two blocks from downtown.
→ Fact + 3rd person singular → lives.
6. Tomorrow morning we ___ pick up the keys — the lease is already signed.
→ Уже спланировано (lease signed) → going to.
7. I think this neighborhood ___ feel like home in a month or two.
→ "I think" → subjective prediction → will.
8. At the moment we ___ for a new drugstore near the office.
→ "at the moment" → Continuous → are looking.
9. The city ___ open a new subway line next year — I read it in the paper.
→ Есть evidence (news) → уже планируют → is going to.
10. On chilly mornings we usually ___ to the coffee shop before work.
→ "on … mornings" + "usually" → habit → Simple · walk. NOT "are walking" — не про этот момент.
11. Wait! I ___ show you the directions on my phone.
→ Spontaneous offer в момент разговора → will.
12. Look! She ___ for the transit pass office right now.
→ "Look!" → this moment → Continuous · is looking.

07

Café dialogue · Can / Could / Like / Prefer · 8 choices

Same café phrases you already know. Choose the polite / correct option.

Register: Can I = ok · Could I / May I = more polite. I like = general. I would prefer / I'd prefer = choose one over another.
8 choices · café + polite forms
1. ___ I have a flat white to go, please?
→ Polite request → Could I have.
2. I ___ the small coffee shop to the big chain across the street.
→ "prefer X to Y" = ставим одно выше другого → prefer.
3. Do you ___ maple syrup in your latte?
→ После Do you — базовая форма → like.
4. ___ I pay with the transit pass here?
→ Разрешение / возможность → Can I.
5. Sorry, the drugstore ___ that brand of vitamin D today.
→ Отрицание к has got (3rd person) → hasn't got.
6. I ___ taking the subway to driving downtown.
→ После prefer → -ing вариант ("prefer taking") без "to". «prefer to» ≠ то же самое.
7. ___ I ask you for directions to the drugstore?
→ Просьба у прохожего → Can I.
8. I don't ___ the subway at rush hour — it's too crowded.
→ После don't — базовая форма → like.
08

Future · will vs going to · 8 sentences

The freshest topic (28 June). Will = decision at the moment / prediction. Going to = plan already made / obvious from what you see.

Quick rule: "OK, I will help you" (decide now) · "I am going to study English tomorrow" (already planned). "Look at the sky — it is going to rain" (evidence).
8 questions · will vs going to
1. — It's chilly in here. — OK, I ___ turn the heating up.
→ Решение в момент разговора → will.
2. Next weekend we ___ sign the lease — the landlord already emailed the papers.
→ Заранее спланировано → are going to.
3. Look how packed the subway platform is — we ___ miss this train.
→ Очевидное по фактам (толпа) → are going to.
4. I think our neighborhood ___ get a lot quieter after the summer.
→ Прогноз / мнение → will ("I think").
5. On Saturday morning we ___ move into the new flat — the truck is booked.
→ План уже принят (truck booked) → are going to.
6. Wait! I ___ grab the directions from my phone for you.
→ Спонтанное предложение в момент → will.
7. The drugstore ___ open a second branch downtown — I saw the "coming soon" sign.
→ Уже планируют (evidence — знак) → is going to.
8. Probably the coffee shop ___ be crowded on Sunday morning.
→ "Probably" → предположение / прогноз → will.
09

Frequency & prepositions · 8 sentences

Two things at once. Frequency words stand BEFORE the main verb (but AFTER to be). Prepositions of time: at 8 o'clock · in the morning · on weekends · during the day.

Position: I usually drink coffee (before verb) · I am usually tired (after to be) · Usually, I drink coffee (comma, at start).
8 questions · frequency + at/in/on/during
1. I ___ catch the subway around 7:30 a.m.
→ Frequency word ПЕРЕД main verb → usually catch.
2. The landlord ___ home in the evenings.
→ Frequency word ПОСЛЕ to be → is often.
3. I meet my colleagues at the coffee shop ___ 8:15 a.m.
→ Точное время → at.
4. I usually walk to the drugstore ___ the afternoon.
→ Часть дня → in the morning / afternoon / evening.
5. We explore the neighborhood ___ the weekends.
→ Дни / weekends → on.
6. The subway is packed ___ rush hour.
→ "rush hour" / "day" / "night" (продолжительный отрезок) → during.
7. I ___ use my transit pass on weekends — it's for weekdays only.
→ Frequency word ПЕРЕД глаголом → rarely (rare = прилагательное).
8. The subway starts running ___ 6 in the morning.
→ Точное время (6) → at.

Ordinals + Cardinals · match + gap-fill

Cardinal = how many (one, two, three). Ordinal = which one in a row (first, second, third). Two short drills — match, then fill.

Rule: 1st = first · 2nd = second · 3rd = third — memorise these three. From 4 onwards, add -th: four → fourth, five → fifth (careful — drop the e), nine → ninth (also drop the e), twelve → twelfth, twenty → twentieth. Ordinals almost always take the: the first day, the third stop.
