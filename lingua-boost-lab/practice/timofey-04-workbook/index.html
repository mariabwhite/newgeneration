3rd person -s · 15 предложений (MCQ)

He / she / it / имя / компания — глагол получает -s. Один клик → зелёный/красный фидбек + пояснение.

15 items · pick the right form
1. My boss ___ in Milan.
→ boss (he) → works.
2. The team ___ every Monday at 10.
→ team (it) → meets.
3. She ___ her email every morning.
→ she → checks.
4. Our company ___ logistics.
→ company (it) → covers.
5. Marco ___ the report on Fridays.
→ Marco → prepares.
6. He usually ___ home at seven.
→ he → goes (o → oes).
7. The boss ___ call me on Sundays.
→ he/she/it → doesn't + инфинитив.
8. She ___ English well.
→ she → speaks.
9. The negotiation ___ three hours on average.
→ negotiation (it) → takes.
10. He ___ hard every day.
→ try → tries (y → ies после согласной).
11. My schedule ___ every quarter.
→ schedule (it) → changes.
12. The client ___ a fast response.
→ client (he/it) → expects.
13. Our team ___ shipments every day.
→ team (it) → checks.
14. It usually ___ me two hours.
→ it → takes.
15. The company ___ ten regions in Russia.
→ company (it) → covers.

06

Present Simple vs Continuous · 8 MCQ

Рутина или прямо сейчас? Смотри на сигналы (every / usually · right now / at the moment).

8 items
1. Every morning I ___ a coffee.
→ every morning → Simple.
2. Right now Marco ___ the elaboration.
→ right now → Continuous.
3. My boss ___ in Milan.
→ факт → Simple.
4. My team ___ a presentation this week.
→ this week (временное) → Continuous.
5. The company usually ___ shipments every Friday.
→ usually + every → Simple.
6. Look! He ___ a new suit today.
→ Look! → Continuous.
7. She ___ English very well.
→ навык / факт → Simple.
8. At the moment we ___ a new supplier.
→ at the moment → Continuous.
9. I always ___ a coffee before the first meeting.
→ always + habit → Simple.
10. Look — the boss ___ a phone call in the corridor.
→ Look — прямо сейчас → Continuous.
07

Present Perfect vs Perfect Continuous · 8 MCQ

Результат сейчас или процесс всё ещё идёт? Смотри на сигналы (already / just — Perfect · for / since / lately — Perfect Continuous).

8 items
1. Today I ___ three submissions.
→ результат сегодня → Perfect.
2. I ___ on this deal for two weeks.
→ for two weeks → процесс → Perfect Continuous.
3. The boss has just ___ our schedule.
→ just + результат → Perfect.
4. Sales ___ since January.
→ since + процесс → Perfect Continuous.
5. I ___ your message.
→ результат → Perfect.
6. They ___ the interview since 2 p.m.
→ since 2 p.m. → процесс → Perfect Continuous.
7. She has already ___ the invoice.
→ already → результат → Perfect.
8. I ___ here for one year.
→ for one year + still ongoing → Perfect Continuous.
9. The team ___ the presentation. It is ready.
→ It is ready → результат → Perfect.
10. I ___ a lot of files today, and I am tired.
→ «I am tired» → фокус на процессе → Perfect Continuous.

10

📖 Reading · A day at the office · 200 words + 10 comprehension

Прочитай текст, потом 10 вопросов с выбором ответа. Обрати внимание на глаголы — там 4 времени сразу.

reading · ~200 words · 10 MCQ

My name is Elena and I work in the finance department of a big logistics company. Every morning I arrive at the office at eight thirty. I always have a coffee and check my schedule before the first meeting at nine.

Right now our team is preparing a big presentation for the board. We have been working on it for three weeks. My boss has already approved the first draft, but she wants us to improve the numbers section.

My colleague Marco speaks five languages. He is talking to a foreign client from Italy at the moment. He has been introducing our new services since Monday, and the client seems interested.

I usually go home by underground at six. Yesterday I came home very late because I made two important calls. I had a small ritual — a cup of tea and a book — before I went to bed.

Tomorrow we have a team meeting at ten. I am going to present our progress. I hope the boss will approve everything and we can finally send the presentation.

1. Where does Elena work?
→ «I work in the finance department of a big logistics company.»
2. What time does Elena arrive at the office?
→ «Every morning I arrive at the office at eight thirty.»
3. How long has the team been working on the presentation?
→ «We have been working on it for three weeks.» — Present Perfect Continuous.
4. What has the boss done with the first draft?
→ «My boss has already approved the first draft.» — Present Perfect + already.
5. Which section does the boss want to improve?
→ «She wants us to improve the numbers section.»
6. How many languages does Marco speak?
→ «Marco speaks five languages.»
7. What is Marco doing right now?
→ «He is talking to a foreign client from Italy at the moment.» — Present Continuous.
8. Why did Elena come home late yesterday?
→ «Yesterday I came home very late because I made two important calls.»
9. What was Elena's ritual before bed?
→ «I had a small ritual — a cup of tea and a book — before I went to bed.»
10. What is Elena going to do tomorrow?
→ «I am going to present our progress.» — going to = plan.
11

🎧 Listening · Marco calls Timofey · 90 sec + 10 comprehension

Прослушай диалог Marco и Timofey. Первый раз — общий смысл. Второй раз — детали для вопросов.

listening · 90 seconds · 10 MCQ
📜 Open transcript (only after first listening)

Marco: Hi Timofey! Do you have a minute?

Timofey: Sure, Marco, what is it?

Marco: I have been working on the new schedule for two weeks. The boss has just approved it. Every team meets on Mondays now.

Timofey: That is good news. My team is preparing a presentation about the internal system. We are trying to improve the process.

Marco: Excellent. When do you have your next meeting?

Timofey: I have a meeting at three p.m. today. I usually have a coffee before it. Do you want to join?

Marco: I would love to, but I am leaving the office at two. See you tomorrow!

1. How long has Marco been working on the new schedule?
→ «I have been working on the new schedule for two weeks.»
2. Who has approved the schedule?
→ «The boss has just approved it.»
3. When do the teams meet in the new schedule?
→ «Every team meets on Mondays now.»
4. What is Timofey's team preparing?
→ «My team is preparing a presentation about the internal system.»
5. What is Timofey's team trying to do with the process?
→ «We are trying to improve the process.»
6. At what time is Timofey's meeting?
→ «I have a meeting at three p.m. today.»
7. What does Timofey usually have before the meeting?
→ «I usually have a coffee before it.»
8. Does Timofey invite Marco?
→ Timofey: «Do you want to join?»
9. Why can't Marco join?
→ «I am leaving the office at two.»
10. When will they see each other?
→ «See you tomorrow!»

✍️ Writing · describe your Monday · 100–130 words

Полный абзац про твой типичный понедельник. Проверяй себя по чек-листу справа — 8 пунктов. Отправь готовый текст в чат.

writing · 100–130 words · 8-point checklist
0 words · target 100–130checklist: 0/8
Чек-лист · перед тем как отправить в чат, проверь все 8 пунктов:
  1. Все глаголы в Present Simple (без «will start», без «changing»).
  2. После he/she/it/имени/компании — окончание -s.
  3. Артикль an перед гласной («an hour, an interview»).
  4. Устойчивые сочетания без артикля: at work, go home, have breakfast.
  5. Правильные формы неправильных глаголов, если пишешь про вчера: came, made, took, taught, said.
  6. Порядок притяжательных: our new schedule, не «the new our schedule».
  7. Мн. число без артикля: foreign clients, не «a foreign client».
  8. Минимум 6 слов из vocab-блока: breakfast, schedule, negotiation, preparing, working, meeting, upgrade, improve, ritual, underground, railway, supplies, likely, impact, introduce, approve, communication.
