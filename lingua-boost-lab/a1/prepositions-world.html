Why this lesson works

A1 prepositions, built as a visual place game

Picture logic

In, on, under, next to and between are tied to visible positions before learners choose words.

10 playful rounds

Sorting, matching, listening and bonus game mechanics recycle the same small grammar set.

Spatial confidence

Learners move from recognition to short sentences about where objects are.

Fast correction

Immediate checks make location mistakes visible and easy to repair.

World Mission

A little explorer is looking for lost things. Find the objects and say where they are.
