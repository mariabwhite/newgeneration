Easter Traditions

Easter is a spring holiday. It is in March or April.

The date changes because it depends on the moon.

The Easter Bunny is a friendly rabbit. He brings chocolate eggs to children.

Children look for hidden eggs in the garden. This is called an egg hunt!

    8. Easter Customs Flip to learn!

    Easter is celebrated all over the world! Every country has its own special traditions. Some people decorate eggs, some hide them in the garden, and some bake special bread.

    In Germany, people hang painted eggs on Easter trees. In the UK, children roll eggs down a hill. In the USA, the President invites children to the White House for an egg roll! Let's learn more — flip the cards to discover Easter customs!

    Choose One Country and Say

    In Germany, people decorate Easter trees. In the UK, children roll eggs. In the USA, children roll eggs near the White House. In Russia, people paint eggs. In Greece, families dye eggs red. In Italy, people eat colomba cake.
