Learn classroom vocabulary, subject pronouns, spelling and short speaking patterns in one guided A1 lesson.

Why this lesson works

A1 school words and pronouns, built as a classroom toolkit

Useful school set

Classroom objects and school phrases give learners words they can use immediately.

Pronouns in context

I, you, he, she, it, we and they are practised through classroom examples, not isolated charts.

Recognition to output

Cards, choices and short speaking tasks move learners from seeing words to using them.

Gentle game pacing

Small wins, badges and checks keep the A1 workload clear and manageable.
