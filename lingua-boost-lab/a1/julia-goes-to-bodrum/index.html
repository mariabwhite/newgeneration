🏊 The pool race! 🏆

Listening · who came first, second, third?

🏊 Julia and John had a race in the pool! Read, listen, and watch the comic:

🎧 The full dialogue
📖 Read the dialogue

Julia: John, look! The swimming pool is big!

John: Yes! Let's swim!

Julia: Wait… I have an idea!

John: What idea?

Julia: Let's have a race!

John: A race? Really?

Julia: Yes! From here to the end!

John: Okay! I want to win!

Julia: Ready?

John: Ready!

Julia: One… two… three… GO!

John: I am swimming fast!

Julia: I am faster!

John: No, you are not!

Julia: Look! I am first!

John: Oh no! I am second!

Julia: I win!

John: Good job, Julia!

Julia: Thank you! Let's do it again!

John: Yes! One more time!

Answer the questions:

1. Where did they race?
2. Who came FIRST? 🥇
3. Who came SECOND? 🥈
4. Who came THIRD? 🥉
5. Did Mum take a photo?
