🌟 Evening walk under the stars 🌃

🌙 After dinner, Julia and her family walked by the sea. Listen and answer:

🌌 Julia's evening · 50 seconds
Listen — what did Julia see, hear and eat?
📖 Read the story

In the evening, the sun went down. The sky was orange and pink. Julia and her family walked by the sea. The sand was warm under their feet. Mum bought a cup of Turkish tea. John drank cold lemonade. Julia ate a small ice cream — chocolate again! Then they saw the first star. Then the second. Then twenty stars! Julia said: «This is the best holiday in the world!» Then they walked back to the hotel. Julia was sleepy but very happy.

Answer the questions:

1. What colour was the sky?
2. What did Mum buy?
3. What did John drink?
4. Which ice cream did Julia eat?
5. How many stars did they see?
