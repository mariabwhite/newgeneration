Why this lesson works

Past Simple verbs, built as a route adventure

Story route

Tom moves through a journey, so irregular verbs appear as actions in a sequence rather than a list.

Six-stop progression

Each stop adds a focused task, then the tracker shows where the learner is on the route.

Form plus meaning

Find/found, take/took and other pairs are practised inside sentence choices and story recall.

Game feedback

Stars, score and combo moments make repetition feel like progress instead of drilling.

Meet Tom and Sally

Tom and Sally are young explorers. Today they invite you to join their Past Simple Adventure. The route starts at the market, goes past the map table, road, camp and cave, and ends on a tiny island. At every stop you will find verbs, match words, build sentences and unlock the story.
