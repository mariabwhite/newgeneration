03

Drill · Do or Are?

The most common trap. Do — for action verbs (live, go, like, work). Are — for adjectives, professions, location, long -ing forms.

1. ___ you live in the city?
→ live — habitual action → Do you live
2. ___ you walking now?
→ walking (-ing) → Are you walking
3. ___ you like coffee?
→ like — habitual action → Do you like
4. ___ they at home?
→ at home — location (where they are) → Are they at home
5. ___ she work in the centre?
→ work — action (she = 3rd person → Does) → Does she work
6. ___ she a doctor?
→ profession (a doctor) → Is she a doctor
7. ___ they cooking dinner?
→ cooking (-ing) → Are they cooking
8. ___ he drive a car?
→ drive — action → Does he drive
04

Drill · Present Simple or Continuous?

Simple — what's usual, regular, always. Markers: every day, usually, often, sometimes, never. Continuous — what's happening right now. Markers: now, at the moment, look!

1. Right now, she ___ in the park.
→ right now → Continuous → is walking
2. I usually ___ coffee in the morning.
→ usually → Simple → drink
3. Look! It ___ outside.
→ Look! = right now → Continuous → is raining
4. He ___ to work every day.
→ every day → Simple → walks (3rd person + s)
5. They ___ a movie at the moment.
→ at the moment → Continuous → are watching
6. She always ___ her keys at home.
→ always → Simple → forgets
7. "Where is John?" — "He ___ dinner now."
→ now → Continuous → is cooking
8. I never ___ milk in my coffee.
→ never → Simple → put
05

Article · the with places

Simple rule: the + specific place (the bank, the park, the museum). No article — work, home, school, bed (when it's the routine, not the building).

1
I go to park every Sunday.
2
She goes to work at 9 a.m. (no article — type a long dash —)
3
The shop is opposite bank.
4
He stays at home in the evening.
5
My children go to school by bus.
6
Wait for me at bus stop next to the café.
7
She is sitting in kitchen now.
8
We go to bed late on Fridays.

Remember the pattern: work, home, school, bed — no the (when we talk about the activity, not the building). All other specific places — the: the park, the bank, the museum, the post office, the corner, the bus stop, the café.

Hostel admin · True or False

Read the dialogue between guest and hostel admin. Then choose True / False.

Guest: Hi! Is there a free double room on the 4th of June?

Admin: Oh, I'm afraid there are no double rooms for this date. But there are free beds in a room for eight people.

Guest: Oh really? That's bad. OK. We want to book two beds in that room.

Admin: Great! Now I need some details ...

Guest: Um, is it far from the city centre?

Admin: No, it's just a ten-minute walk from here.

Guest: So ... what does your hostel offer?

Admin: First, there's free Wi-Fi everywhere. Then, there are lockers, a television, a hair dryer, an iron and air conditioning in every room. There are also some vending machines on the first floor.

Guest: Is there a kitchen?

Admin: Sorry, no. But there are lots of nice restaurants around.

1. The hostel has free double rooms on 4 June.

2. The hostel is a ten-minute walk from the city centre.

3. There is free Wi-Fi everywhere in the hostel.

4. The hostel has a kitchen for guests.

Tenses · all we know · 12 sentences

Three tense groups in one set: Present Simple vs Continuous (rules of city/hotel life vs what's happening now), Will vs Going to (spontaneous vs planned future).

📐 Quick rules · will vs going to

Will → spontaneous
deciding right now
— I'm thirsty.
— I'll get you some water.
Will → predictions
I think / maybe
I think the hotel will be quiet at night.
Going to → planned
already decided
I'm going to stay at the Grand View — I booked it yesterday.
Going to → evidence
I can see it now
Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.

4. I usually ___ breakfast at 8 a.m.

5. Look! The bellboy ___ your bags upstairs right now.
