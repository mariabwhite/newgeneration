03 Focus A: there is / there are

Use there is for one object and there are for several. It helps describe what exists in a country, city, or room.

FormSingularPlural
StatementThere is a Great Wall.There are many pandas.
NegativeThere isn't a beach in Beijing.There aren't tigers in cities.
QuestionIs there a metro in Shanghai?Are there pandas in the zoo?

Typical mistake

There is many temples. → There are many temples.

04 Focus B: bigger, the biggest, usually

Three forms in one table: compare two places, point out the most extreme, describe what locals usually do.

FormShort wordsLong wordsIrregular
ComparativeBeijing is bigger than Xi'an.Shanghai is more modern than Lhasa.Tea is better known than coffee.
SuperlativeThe Great Wall is the longest wall in the world.The Forbidden City is the most famous palace in China.The Terracotta Army is the best example of imperial art.
HabitsPeople drink tea every day. A panda eats bamboo. We usually visit temples in spring.

Typical mistakes

Shanghai is more bigger than Xi'an. → Shanghai is bigger than Xi'an.

A panda eat bamboo. → A panda eats bamboo. (habits: -s after he / she / it)
