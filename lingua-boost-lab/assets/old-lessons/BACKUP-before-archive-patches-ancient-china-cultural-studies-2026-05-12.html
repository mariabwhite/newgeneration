Ancient Ideas, Modern World

Many ideas from Ancient China still influence modern life. Paper and printing changed education because information became easier to record and share. The compass changed travel and trade. Philosophies such as Confucianism and Daoism shaped ideas about family, responsibility, balance, and nature. Today, these ideas are not only history; they are part of how people understand culture, identity, and progress.

Answer the gist question and details.

Discovery Giant Pandas · National Treasure bonus comprehension

Giant pandas live only in China. Fewer than 2,000 remain in the wild. They spend many hours each day eating bamboo, and China has created special reserves to protect them. Pandas are now a global symbol of wildlife conservation and cultural identity.
