Hello! Classroom Fun

10 friendly games for the very first English lesson — greetings, actions, basic phrases, prepositions and pronouns.

Pre-A1 10 Activities 25–35 min Speaking

Why this lesson works

Pre-A1 classroom language, built as a first adventure

First-contact English

Greetings, names and classroom phrases are introduced as friendly routines children can repeat safely.

Audio plus action

Listen buttons, comic panels and Simon Says connect language with movement and response.

10 micro-activities

Short rounds keep attention fresh while still covering speaking, listening and recognition.

Low-pressure output

The lesson moves from tapping and copying to small spoken phrases, so beginners can join in fast.
