10 garden games to grow body words and clear grammar: This is, That is, I have, She has.

Pre-A1 10 Activities 20-25 min Speaking Grammar: have / this-that

Why this lesson works

Pre-A1 grammar garden, built as a playful body lab

Body vocabulary first

Children meet body words through cards, memory and listening before they need to produce full phrases.

Grammar in tiny moves

Have / has and this / that are practised through short choices, not long explanations.

10 activity path

Flashcards, sentence building, memory and whack-a-mole keep the lesson moving without overload.

Visible progress

Stars, garden friends and quick feedback make each completed activity feel like a small win.
